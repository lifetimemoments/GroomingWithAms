Hi, I'm Amy. Grooming With Ams is a one-to-one grooming studio in Worthing, a calm little salon where your dog gets my full attention from the moment they walk in.

With over four years of experience, I noticed too many salons focus on speed over care. So I built something different: no cages, no conveyor belt of dogs, no chaos. Every groom is tailored to your dog's breed, coat and temperament, using premium, hypoallergenic, eco-friendly products.

Whether it's a full groom or a nervous pup's very first visit, they're treated like one of my own. Because here, they're not just pets, they're part of the family.

Good to know.

The honest small print, so there are no surprises on grooming day.

Fleas

Dogs with fleas carry a £20 surcharge. Please let Amy know if your dog has (or recently had) fleas, a flea bath will be given at the groom, and flea-positive dogs are booked as the last groom of the day so the parlour can be deep-cleaned with zero cross-contamination.

Matting

Matted coats carry a £30 surcharge. For your dog's own safety and welfare (Animal Welfare Act 2006), severe matting can't be brushed out, it must be clipped off. Mats trap dirt and parasites, pull on the skin causing sores and hair loss, and can hide hot spots that may need vet attention.

Cancellations & lateness

Life happens, appointments can always be moved. Under 24 hours' notice or a no-show is charged at 100% of the groom; 48 hours is 50%. There's a 15-minute grace period: if you'll be later, let Amy know and a bath & tidy or dry cut may be offered instead (still full price).

Photos & social media

Amy takes photos and videos during grooms for the Grooming With Ams socials. If you'd rather your pup stayed off the grid, just say so.

Payment & extras

Cash or bank transfer (BACS before collection). Shampoo and perfume are available to buy in-salon. Treats are used throughout, please flag any allergies. Fully qualified, fully insured, first-aid trained.
